How a flag gets made

Four steps, all by hand, in the order they happen.

  1. 01

    Cut & shape

    Planks are milled to size and routed to follow the wave of the fabric.

  2. 02

    Sand & seal

    Every edge is broken by hand and the grain is sealed before paint.

  3. 03

    Paint & finish

    Canton and stripes are painted by hand, then sealed in satin or matte.

  4. 04

    Pack & ship

    Mounting hardware goes in the box, and it ships within 7–10 business days.
